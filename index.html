Delta Solution Office · Talent Strategy Refresh

從「條件堆疊」到「角色聚焦」

這次 JD 調整的核心，不是降低標準，而是保留真正關鍵能力、放寬非必要交集， 並把每個角色的 ownership 與 career proposition 說清楚，讓對的人更願意投。

3 個關鍵職缺
Program · Site Integration · Service Operations
為什麼要調整 優化邏輯 職缺調整總覽 Program Manager Site Integration Service Operations
01 · Why change

履歷少，不代表市場沒人；三個職缺其實是三種不同問題

目前三個職缺的 applicant funnel 都偏小，但原因不同。若只用同一種方式「放寬條件」， 會失去原本需要的能力；因此這次採用 role-specific 的調整方式。

1

Program Manager：Pool Problem

Microgrid / DCIM domain 條件太窄，讓很多有能力的 Technical PM、Energy PM、SI PM 在投遞前就自我淘汰。

2

Site Integration：Intersection Problem

若同時要求 commissioning、OT、DCIM、EMS、BMS、SCADA、protocol、training，人才交集會非常小。

3

Service Operations：Story Problem

候選人不是不具備能力，而是不清楚「Delta 為什麼需要這個角色、進來能 build 什麼、未來會長成什麼」。

原本容易出現的問題

  • Domain requirement 被候選人理解成 hard gate
  • JD 同時涵蓋太多不同專業能力
  • 大量使用 support / coordinate，ownership 不夠清楚
  • 看得出工作內容，但看不出加入後的職涯價值

新的設計原則

  • 保留真正 must-have，其他移到 preferred
  • 把「AND」條件改成「one or more of」
  • 強調 outcome ownership，而不是行政協調
  • 清楚說明 0→1、Cross-BG、regional scale 的挑戰與影響力
02 · Optimization logic

不是放寬要求，而是讓條件更像「人才市場看得懂的工作」

Keep the critical capabilities as must-haves, while avoiding unnecessary overlap that significantly narrows the candidate pool.

Senior Solution Program Manager – Data Center & Energy

Location · Taipei, Taiwan

Role Overview

We are looking for a Senior Solution Program Manager to drive the execution of cross-BG solution projects from defined requirements through pilot deployment.

This is not a software development PM role. Your focus is end-to-end solution execution, dependency management, requirement alignment, site readiness, and issue closure.

What You Will Own

  • Drive projects from scope alignment through pilot and go-live
  • Manage milestones, dependencies, risks, actions, and deliverables
  • Coordinate BG, IT, architects, domain teams, vendors, and site stakeholders
  • Facilitate requirement clarification and decision follow-up
  • Coordinate SIT, UAT, pilot readiness, and issue resolution

Required

  • 5+ years in Project / Program / Solution Management, Technical PM, or System Integration
  • Strong cross-functional project execution
  • Ability to understand software applications, interfaces, and technical discussions
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication
  • Customer- or site-facing project experience
  • Fluent in English and Chinese

Preferred

Data Center Energy EMS BESS Microgrid DCIM BMS / SCADA
  • Site deployment / commissioning / system integration
  • APIs, OT systems, cloud / edge platforms
招聘訊息重點：Execution is mandatory. Domain is a strong plus.

Site Integration & Commissioning Manager

Location · Taipei, Taiwan

Role Overview

We are looking for an experienced Site Integration & Commissioning Manager to lead the deployment and validation of our Cross-BG solution projects.

You will bridge solution design and field execution. You do not need to perform installation work yourself, but you must have strong field experience and be able to communicate effectively with both OT and software teams.

What You Will Own

  • Lead site survey, readiness assessment, integration planning, SIT, commissioning, and validation
  • Coordinate SI teams, BG engineers, IT / software teams, vendors, and site stakeholders
  • Review OT network, device connectivity, gateways, protocols, interfaces, and prerequisites
  • Support data-point mapping, interface clarification, and troubleshooting
  • Drive technical issues toward closure
  • Maintain integration documents, test plans, and commissioning records

Required

  • 7+ years in system integration, commissioning, industrial automation, site deployment, or technical project delivery
  • Strong field experience with complex multi-system environments
  • Solid understanding of OT systems, networking, equipment integration, and commissioning
  • Strong troubleshooting, coordination, and stakeholder management
  • Fluent in English and Chinese

Preferred

Data Center DCIM / EMS Microgrid BMS / SCADA Industrial Automation Power / Energy
  • Modbus, BACnet, OPC UA, REST API, gateways, industrial networking
  • DCIM / EMS / industrial software deployment
  • SIT / UAT and cross-system troubleshooting
調整重點：拿掉 training / enablement 作為招聘條件；保留「懂現場、懂整合、能與 SI 充分溝通」三個核心。

Solution Operations & Service Strategy Manager

Location · Taipei, Taiwan

Role Overview

Delta is expanding from hardware products toward software-enabled and recurring solution businesses. We are looking for a Solution Operations & Service Strategy Manager to help design how these new solutions should be operated, supported, governed, and scaled globally.

This is a 0-to-1 role. You will help build the operating model and work with Delta’s existing IT, Service, BG, and regional organizations to make it real.

What You Will Own

  • Define the operating model for software-enabled and cross-BG solutions
  • Establish service ownership, support tiers, escalation paths, and cross-functional R&R
  • Define standards for SLA, incident, problem, change, release, and lifecycle management
  • Establish service-readiness criteria before launch
  • Build reusable playbooks, KPIs, governance mechanisms, and operating processes
  • Help shape and eventually lead a future Solution Service & Operations capability

Required

  • 7+ years in SaaS / Software Operations, Solution Operations, Service Strategy, Customer Success Operations, or related roles
  • Experience designing or improving enterprise software operating models
  • Strong knowledge of SLA, incident / problem / change / release management
  • Proven ability to build processes across multiple organizations
  • Strong business and stakeholder-management capability
  • Fluent in English and Chinese

Preferred

  • Experience from leading SaaS, enterprise software, cloud, or digital-solution companies
  • Experience at Siemens, Schneider Electric, or similar industrial-digital companies
  • Experience building a new operation or service-management function from the ground up
招聘訊息重點：Build the operating model, not just operate an existing service.
Closing message

這次調整，不是把門檻降低，而是把門檻放在真正重要的地方。

目標是讓 candidate 更清楚：自己為什麼適合、進來要 own 什麼、以及這個角色在 Delta solution transformation 裡會造成什麼影響。
